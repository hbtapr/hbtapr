# Hebert Antunes
**Full-stack Developer · AI Automation**

I build SaaS products and run AI agents in production, from the database schema to the WhatsApp message the customer receives. Today I lead product development and own infrastructure, deploys and AI operations at **Agência Toss**, a marketing agency for gyms and fitness studios. I'm also available for freelance projects.

---

## What I build

* **Toss CRM** · Multi-tenant SaaS for gyms and studios: lead pipeline with follow-up cadences, trial class booking, class schedules and check-ins, subscription billing via Asaas, and Meta/Google Ads metrics with Conversions API events.
  > `Laravel 12` `Livewire 4` `PHP 8.4` `Pest`

* **AI agents on WhatsApp** · Sales and scheduling assistants in production for 20+ businesses, booking trial classes and updating leads directly in the CRM.
  > `n8n (queue mode)` `OpenAI` `Redis` `uazapi`

* **AI quality monitoring** · Every agent conversation is mirrored into a self-hosted Chatwoot (~11k conversations in the last 3 months). An LLM reviews them every 2 hours, a daily audit checks every workflow, and the team gets alerts on WhatsApp.
  > `Python` `Chatwoot` `OpenAI` `Claude` `Supabase`

* **Pipe** · The agency's internal project and task management system, with Kanban pipelines and an API consumed by the automations.
  > `Next.js 15` `React 19` `TypeScript` `Prisma` `NextAuth`

* **Agendia** *(side project)* · White-label scheduling CRM for law firms, health clinics and therapists, with AI customer service via n8n.
  > `Laravel 12` `Livewire 4` `PostgreSQL` `Asaas`

<br>

🔭 **Currently exploring:** LoRA fine-tuning of open-weight models to self-host our agents.
